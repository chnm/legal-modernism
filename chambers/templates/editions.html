Editions: pipeline and LLM citations

Every edition in the treatise view, US and UK, with the citations each method found in it. The pipeline is the regex detector and the linker, run over the whole corpus. The LLM reading is Claude reading an edition page by page and linking each case citation by its own judgement (issue #439), done so far for the editions of Stephen on Pleading. Editions with an LLM reading are listed first. Citations are every citation found; linked is the share that reached a case; cases are the distinct cases reached.

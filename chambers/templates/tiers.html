Match tiers

How far the linker got with each citation. The status says whether a citation linked; the match tier says which probe produced the link, or which step of the cascade ran out of things to match against. Citations turned away before any probe (skipped_junk, skipped_not_whitelisted) carry no tier and are not counted here. The linking dashboard has the same figures as tables.

Citations by tier

One bar per tier, grouped by status. The label gives the count and the tier's share of its status, not of the corpus.

Tiers by reporter

One bar per reporter_standard, heaviest first. Share puts every reporter on the same 100% so the shapes compare; count shows how much each tier weighs. Hover a segment for its count. Click a reporter to see its raw citations.

SQL queries 
SELECT status, match_tier, n, pct_of_status
FROM moml_citations.linking_tier_summary
ORDER BY n DESC;

SELECT reporter_standard, COALESCE(status, 'unprocessed'), COALESCE(match_tier, ''), n
FROM moml_citations.linking_dashboard_tiers
ORDER BY sum(n) OVER (PARTITION BY reporter_standard) DESC, reporter_standard, n DESC;

Both read moml_citations.linking_dashboard_tiers, a materialized view refreshed by make db-maintenance. Every figure on this page is as of that last refresh, not as of the last linker run.

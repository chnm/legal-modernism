Chambers

The citations that the treatises of the Making of Modern Law make to reported cases: found by the detector, resolved by the linker, and browsable here by the entities they connect.

Treatises

The works in the treatise view, US and UK, from a work to its editions, to each volume's pages, to the citations on a page in the order they appear. Each work and edition shows the cases it cites most.

Cases

The cases the treatises cite, from the Caselaw Access Project, the English Reports, the Code Reporter, and the stub cases no source holds, ranked by the editions that cite them. Each case shows the editions that cite it and the spellings they used.

Reporters

The series of law reports the treatises cite: their spellings, how their citations fared in linking, the cases reached through them, and the editions that cite them most.

Citations

The individual citations: find them by a cite string, a reporter, an edition, or a case, and inspect one in full, from the page it was found on to the case it linked to.

Detecting & linking

How the linker fared over the whole corpus: the dashboard, the match tiers charted by reporter, and the whitelist extender for the spellings it does not know yet. The same for the citations in the CAP opinions, set against CAP's own citation graph case by case.
